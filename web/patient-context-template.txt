SCRIPTSYNC PATIENT CONTEXT
==========================

Fill in only what's relevant to your question, then paste this into the
"Patient context" box before you ask. Delete any line you don't need.

This is used only for the single question you're about to ask, then
discarded. It is never saved, logged, or sent anywhere except to the label
agents answering that one question.

DO NOT INCLUDE:
  - Patient name, initials, or date of birth
  - Medical record number, insurance ID, or any other identifier
  - Anything that could identify who this patient is

DO INCLUDE (only if relevant to your question):
  - Age range or life stage (e.g. "70-80", "elderly", "pediatric, age 8")
  - Renal function (e.g. "normal", "mild impairment", "severe impairment", "dialysis")
  - Liver function (e.g. "normal", "moderate-severe impairment")
  - Pregnancy or breastfeeding status, if applicable
  - Current medications (drug names only)
  - Other relevant history, in your own words - still no identifiers
