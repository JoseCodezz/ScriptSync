FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it tell me what to prescribe?

No. It never recommends a drug, dose, or treatment. Overlap notes are cross-references for clinician review.

Are these real manufacturer agents?

No. They are our demo agents serving public label text, and nothing here is operated by a manufacturer.

Is the identity check real yet?

Not yet. ANS verification is simulated in this demo, and signatures use a demo key.

Does it handle patient data?

No patient data or PHI is used anywhere. Questions that look like they contain patient identifiers are refused and never logged.

Know who's talking.

Signed sources, quoted text, and a journal of every event.
